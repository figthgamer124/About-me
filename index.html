Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How do I invite the bot to my server?
Click the "Add to Discord" button at the top of the page. Select your server, grant the requested permissions, and click "Authorize".
Why isn't the bot responding to commands?
Ensure the bot has the correct channel permissions (`View Channel`, `Send Messages`, `Embed Links`). Also verify if the bot is currently undergoing maintenance.
Is Fightgamer124 free to use?
Yes! All core moderation, logging, and utility features are 100% free for all servers with optional premium features.
What is the infrastructure uptime SLA?
The service is continuously monitored and hosted on dedicated infrastructure designed for 99.9% uptime.
